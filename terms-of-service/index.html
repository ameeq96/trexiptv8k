Trex purchasing and use: key terms

Trex purchases use the shared Opplex service and account systems. This page summarises the central Terms of Service; read the linked full terms and the details shown at checkout before confirming an order.

Your order and renewal

Provide accurate details and check the selected period and total on Opplexify. Plans do not renew automatically. At expiry, review the current offer and decide whether to purchase again.

Responsible access

Keep credentials private and respect copyright, regional restrictions and the plan's limits. Retail access must not be modified, resold or redistributed. Misuse or fraud can lead to suspension or cancellation.

Availability and liability

Device, network and third-party app conditions can affect use. Maintenance or technical downtime may occur. The central terms address indirect losses and cap liability at the subscription amount paid in the previous three months, subject to rights that applicable law does not allow to be excluded.

Final sale and help

Once the price is agreed and the order confirmed, the purchase is final except where a refund is legally required. Review the refund policy and contact support if a term is unclear before paying.

Confirm your device, location and the selected plan before paying. Availability and performance can vary.
