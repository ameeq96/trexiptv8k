Trex questions before you press play

It is easier to choose a plan when the limits are clear. These answers cover the questions that affect a typical television setup; support can check anything specific to your equipment or location.

Will everything play in 8K?

No. Trex IPTV 8K is the brand name, not a guarantee of source resolution. A high-resolution screen cannot add detail that the source does not contain.

Can I use several screens?

Follow the connection allowance shown for your chosen plan. Installing a player on several devices does not establish permission for simultaneous use.

Why does checkout open Opplexify?

Opplexify handles the payment step for these plans. Review the selected service, amount and order details there before confirming; your customer account remains in the central Opplex portal.

Before you buy: a quick device checklist
  • Note your exact device model and operating system.
  • Check the player app and any separate app licence fee.
  • Confirm the selected plan's simultaneous connection allowance.
  • Ask support to confirm your intended setup and location. A device name alone is not a compatibility guarantee.
