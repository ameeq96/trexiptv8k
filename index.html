Before you choose

Think through the year.
Start with your setup.

Choosing a twelve-month IPTV subscription is easier when you know what you need from it. This page lists the current Trex IPTV 8K yearly option from the Opplex IPTV catalogue. Prices and plan details load from that catalogue; your order continues through the central website to Opplexify checkout. Take a moment to check what matters to your household before paying.

Three things to confirm first

A yearly plan is a longer commitment than a short test. Start with the screen you actually use, the content you want to watch and how many people need access at once. A familiar device name alone does not answer all three.

  • Your exact setup. Share your device model, operating system and intended player with support. Ask whether that combination works and whether a separate app or app licence is needed.
  • Your viewing priorities. Ask about the specific channels or languages important to you and availability in your location. Do not assume a brand name or broad catalogue description confirms an individual channel.
  • Your connections. Explain whether you plan to watch on one screen or several at the same time. Confirm the plan's simultaneous connection limit and any extra cost before choosing.

Before you commit

Does “8K” in the name guarantee 8K playback?

No. Trex IPTV 8K is the brand name used on this page, not a promise that streams are available in 8K. Playback quality depends on the source, service, player, device, display and connection. Ask support what is available for your intended setup rather than buying on the name alone.

What if I am still testing my setup?

Resolve compatibility questions before committing to a year. Ask support whether a suitable way to check the service is currently available. Do not assume a free trial, cancellation option or refund is included; read the current terms and ask about anything unclear.

What should I check on the order screen?

Confirm the selected plan, its full duration, currency, final total and any fees. Check the customer name and email carefully, too. Keep your payment confirmation and order reference so support can identify the purchase if you need help later.
